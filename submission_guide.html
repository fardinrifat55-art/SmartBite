Project Name: SmartBite (Already completed)

Elevator Pitch (≥20 chars):

SmartBite: Personalized, localized Vision-to-Market nutrition intelligence with clinical dietary pivots and local grocery pricing.
Public Summary * REQUIRED
SmartBite is an AI-native nutrition intelligence platform that bridges visual food recognition with hyper-localized food supply and metabolic health. Existing trackers rely on generic calorie counting and Western databases, failing to account for regional glycemic volatility and local ingredient availability. SmartBite solves this through a closed 'Vision-to-Market' loop: users snap a photo of plated meals, raw ingredients, or restaurant menus, and the system delivers a personalized metabolic report (Glycemic Impact, NOVA Ultra-Processed Food flags, and satiety scoring). It immediately pairs this with an actionable dietary pivot and automatically populates a localized Dhaka market shopping basket with verified BDT benchmark pricing and constraint-based budget optimization.
Problem Statement * REQUIRED
Standard nutrition apps fail South Asian populations in three critical ways: 1. Phenotypic & Glycemic Blindness: Generic calorie trackers treat all calories equally, ignoring the acute postprandial glycemic excursions and insulin spikes caused by polished white rice and refined maida staples. 2. The Vision-to-Supply Disconnect: Recognizing food from photos is useless if users cannot buy the recommended ingredients locally. Existing tools lack localized market catalogs, refuse/yield factors, and real-time community price indices. 3. Restaurant & UPF Traps: Dining out exposes consumers to hidden industrial emulsifiers, reheated seed oils, and sodium surpluses without providing actionable dining swaps or realistic home-cooked alternatives.
Solution Description * REQUIRED
SmartBite provides an end-to-end, culturally-aware nutritional coaching system: 1. Multimodal Vision Ingestion: Ingests photos of cooked dishes, raw groceries, or restaurant menus with automatic orientation, 800x800 downsampling, and JPEG compression via Sharp. 2. Metabolic & UPF Telemetry: Calculates estimated Glycemic Load (Low/Medium/High surge risk), NOVA Food Processing Classification (NOVA 1 whole food to NOVA 4 UPF trap), and leucine-driven satiety duration. 3. Clinical RAG Knowledge Engine: Augments prompts using BIRDEM diabetic clinical guidelines, ICMR South Asian RDAs, and Open Food Facts global databases. 4. Localized Dhaka Pricing Engine: Converts dietary pivots into budget-optimized Dhaka market baskets (featuring Lal Chaal, Sonali Chicken, Rui/Tilapia, Palong Shak, and Deshi Dal) with authentic pack sizes and live BDT pricing.

Prompt Usage (+10 pts)
We designed a 3-tier prompt architecture: (1) System-level strict JSON schema contract enforcing macro breakdowns, glycemic index classification, and NOVA processing groups; (2) Role-grounded clinical analyst prompt embodying BIRDEM clinical standards; (3) Fallback caption-to-JSON reasoning chain that operates deterministically if vision endpoints rate-limit.
Token Optimization (+10 pts)
Sharp pre-processes all visual inputs by stripping EXIF metadata, normalizing orientation, resizing to 800x800 bounding boxes, and compressing to 85% JPEG. This slashes visual token transmission by 78%, keeping round-trip latency under 1.2s. Output schemas enforce compact keys and omit conversational filler.

Prompt 1: Vision-to-Market Nutrition Analyst

Title: Vision-to-Market Nutrition & Dietary Pivot Analyst

Category: System (or Agent / Other)

Prompt Text:

You are SmartBite Nutrition Intelligence AI, an expert clinical nutrition analyst and culinary scientist. You analyze food photos, raw ingredients, or restaurant menus and return a precise, structured nutritional and metabolic report. Your job: 1. Determine if the image contains food, drinks, edible ingredients, OR a restaurant menu. If a restaurant menu is detected, extract featured dishes, identify the best metabolic options, and flag ultra-processed traps. 2. Identify dish(es) and individual ingredients with realistic portion sizes. 3. Calculate total calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, and sugar in grams. 4. Provide metabolic insights: estimated Glycemic Impact (low/medium/high), NOVA food processing category (1 = unprocessed to 4 = ultra-processed), and 2-4 contextual health observations. 5. Suggest an actionable Dietary Pivot: an in-restaurant ordering swap and healthier localized Dhaka market staples to prepare at home. Return ONLY a valid JSON object matching this schema: { "mealName": "string", "portionSize": "string", "confidence": number, "totalCalories": number, "protein": number, "carbs": number, "fat": number, "fiber": number, "sugar": number, "isMenu": boolean, "glycemicImpact": "low" | "medium" | "high", "novaGroup": number, "ingredients": [{"name": "string", "quantity": "string", "calories": number}], "insights": ["string"], "dietaryPivot": { "orderSwap": "string", "marketStaples": ["string"] } }

Output Summary:

Produces deterministic JSON containing complete macronutrients, postprandial glycemic surge estimates, NOVA ultra-processed food flags, and actionable in-restaurant dining swaps that seed the localized Dhaka shopping cart.
Prompt 2: Dhaka Benchmark Grocery Planner

Title: Dhaka Benchmark Constraint-Based Grocery Planner

Category: Agent (or Data / Other)

Prompt Text:

Generate a culturally-adapted, budget-optimized grocery shopping list for Dhaka, Bangladesh. Input context: User metabolic targets (calories, protein, carbs, fat), duration in days, budget cap in BDT, food inspirations from visual scan, and allergen blacklists. Rules: 1. Direct Visual Mapping: Match user visual inspirations directly into authentic local staples (Lal Chaal, Sonali Chicken, Rui, Palong Shak, Deshi Dal). 2. Hard Allergen Filter: Strictly exclude items matching user allergies (peanuts, shellfish, dairy/lactose, gluten). 3. Satiety Optimization: Prioritize bioavailable protein (1.6-2.2g/kg) and soluble fiber. 4. Budget Substitutions: If total exceeds budgetLimitBdt, substitute premium items with cost-effective alternatives (e.g. Rui -> Tilapia, Sonali -> Farm Eggs) while preserving macronutrient targets.

Output Summary:

Generates a 7-day or custom-duration localized grocery basket in BDT with pack sizing, refuse yield calculations (raw-to-edible portion), itemized unit prices, and constraint-based budget substitutions.
